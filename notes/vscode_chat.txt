My current understanding:

A personal spatial sound playground. You provide a folder of audio samples; the app turns them into looping sound sources arranged in a 2D room. You navigate an oriented listener through that room, hearing volume and stereo position change based on distance and direction. The room has configurable dimensions and reverb.
